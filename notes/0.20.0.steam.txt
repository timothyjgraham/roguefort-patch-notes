[b]Roguefort 0.20.0 — Patch Notes[/b]

This one covers everything since 0.19.6. A hundred and twenty-seven changes, and most of them began as your reports on the Discord.

[b]Spellcasting[/b]
[list]
[*]Manna cap goes past 40. It rises 2 a tier to 40 at fifteen mastery tiers as before, then 1 a tier to 60 at thirty-five. Dungeons still have no refill by design, so the cap alone was never going to make a caster work down there. (horth.panthur)
[*]Mastery cuts what a spell costs to cast. Expert takes 1 off, Master takes 2, floored at half the base cost. A Master tier 3 spell casts for 5, which is twelve casts out of a full 60 pool.
[*]Killing something with a tier 3 mastery spell gives manna back, once per cast, always less than what the cast took. Tier 2 nets 1, tier 3 nets 2, tier 4 nets 3. Tier 1 gets nothing rather than casting for free.
[*]Tier-up messages say what the cap did, and say when it has stopped moving. The Manna bar tooltip explains where the number after the slash comes from. (horth.panthur)
[*]Spells in tileset mode are drawn as pixel art, built from the same hard pixels as the sprites and dithered rather than blurred. Heat stands flames, cold flies shards that stick and creeps rime, rot bubbles and drips into mold, force rolls a cheese wheel into a spoked shockwave, control drifts fog and question marks, arcane orbits runes and stars. All 54 spells, with their own effect for the marquee ones.
[*]2.5D plays those same pixel effects, drawn in the scene rather than flat over the top of it, so a creature standing in a scorch ring draws over the ring.
[*]A blocked FORCE push hurts now. The target takes what stopped it: 1 into something soft, 2 into wood, 3 into stone, 4 into spikes. (nalathnidragon)
[*]Multi-tile creatures are pushed by their whole body. Only the anchor tile was checked, so a 4x4 boss could be shoved three tiles into a wall.
[*]Winter Sovereign grants full cold weather immunity, 10 to 100. The mythic was softening a blizzard's -4 vision to -3, which read as nothing at all. (nalathnidragon)
[*]Speed buffs and bat form work underground. Movement now prices the same in dungeons, the market city and the Plane of Heroes as it does on the overworld, from carried sources only. Attacks and every other action cost a flat turn as before. (rubberychicken)
[*]Kills by poison, burn or a lingering zone carry the mastery picks of whoever cast the thing that killed it.
[*]Every terroir combat roll is seeded.
[/list]

[b]Co-op[/b]
[list]
[*]Each player picks their own mastery upgrades. Casts and tiers stay shared party progression, and only the choice of what a tier buys forks. Host toggle in the pause menu, off by default.
[*]A host can turn friendly fire on and let the party stand in the blast.
[*]Outcome words in the detailed combat log reach both players.
[/list]

[b]The 2.5D view[/b]
[list]
[*]Every actor, tree, prop, item and mount is drawn as the sprite itself, at the size the sprite actually is. No more extruded blocks. A one-tile tree was being drawn 1.08 by 2.2 tiles and then inflated four more times. It is one tile now. (rubberychicken)
[*]The camera orbits. PageUp and PageDown rotate, Home and End tilt, = and - zoom, Insert resets. Middle-drag or Alt+left-drag also orbits, and the right stick looks on a pad. All rebindable.
[*]A compass in the corner of the playfield turns with the camera.
[*]Movement is camera-relative, so walking up-screen walks the way the camera is looking.
[*]Ground is flat in 2.5D, matching the 2D tileset. First person keeps its hills.
[*]Walking starts on the keypress instead of after the turn commits, which is where the reported delay on every step was coming from. (rubberychicken)
[*]Draw calls at spawn fell from 415 to 49, triangles from 252k to 23k, and dynamic meshes from 873 to 54.
[*]Ground geometry is cached per chunk, so dusk does not rebuild every chunk on every turn. Time per rebuild fell from 23.6ms to 17.8ms.
[*]Tilt-shift depth of field blurs the top and bottom of the scene and holds the player's row sharp. High and Epic presets, hardware graphics only.
[*]One HP bar per actor. Two separate draw paths each laid one down, so a damaged creature carried a doubled bar with one copy teleporting and the other following it. (sksniperks)
[*]Doors fade when you stand in the doorway. (sksniperks)
[*]Cloud shadows hold the ground, roads take their tan, and the sea skirt keeps off the beach. (sksniperks)
[/list]

[b]Characters and the title screen[/b]
[list]
[*]Humanoids have a skeleton. The player, townsfolk, workers and co-op partners breathe when idle, stride when walking, tuck and land on a hop, crouch, and swing a strike frame drawn for the weapon they hold. They flinch, guard and dodge when something swings at them.
[*]Your foot and your weapon both lead the way you are moving.
[*]Settings, Gameplay, Character animation turns all of it off for the classic still figures.
[*]The title screen moves. Six clouds drift in parallax between 0.7 and 2 pixels a second, the ships each bob on their own swell, the water throws sparkles, and the hero's robe catches a gust every 7 to 15 seconds. Same art, same framing, cut into planes and put back on the pixel grid.
[/list]

[b]Items and inventory[/b]
[list]
[*]Cosmic backpacks are back. Bag of Folding holds 66 slots at 5,000 gold, Portable Pocket Dimension holds 80 at 10,000. Anyone who bought one in the itch days gets it back on the next load. (nalathnidragon)
[*]Healing cheeses scale with your health. Every pure-heal cheese restores the larger of its flat number and a percentage of your maximum: curds 3%, Lichen Press 4%, Healer's Wheel 5%, Bloomtide Brie 7%, Grand Reserve 8%, Rindwalker's Comte 10%. A 400 HP character's Grand Reserve heals 32 instead of 20, and low levels are unchanged because the flat number still wins. (arkhaven)
[*]Two new top tiers to find. Sagefold Reserve is rare, 30 or 15%, and the Trial Quartermaster stocks one at 80 gold. Heartrind Masterwork is epic, 40 or 20%, and only ever drops.
[*]Two escape cheeses. Farslip Curd blinks you 14 tiles, and Shovewheel pushes every adjacent enemy 2 tiles.
[*]Chest dialogs stop padding your pack out to full capacity. A 38 of 60 pack was drawing 22 Empty rows. (rubberychicken)
[*]Destroy is called Salvage wherever the item can be salvaged, on the button, in the legend and in the right-click menu. (sksniperks)
[*]Hot and local copies of one item fold into a single row, and clicking it opens the real ones. (unknownuluguru_)
[*]Chest items compare against what you are wearing, and so does every other container. (rubberychicken)
[*]A full chest says which stacks it will refuse, and why. (horth.panthur)
[*]Community chests refuse a take your pack has no room for. (loukoumi)
[*]A chest row your pack cannot take greys out before you click it.
[*]Inventory rows keep their name. A wordy refusal ellipsises now instead of erasing it. (rubberychicken)
[*]One purple per rarity everywhere. Fourteen panels were reading a stale cache. (rubberychicken)
[*]Forge upgrades move the price tag as well as the stats, and rings gain stats rather than only a name. (sksniperks)
[*]Auction worth estimates price the whole lot, not one unit of it. (rubberychicken)
[*]Items with no use give you a refusal, and stay in your pack.
[*]Homing whey works from a quick-slot hotkey.
[*]Caltrops are gone. They promised a throw nobody ever wrote.
[/list]

[b]Perks and traditions[/b]
[list]
[*]50 of the 197 perks were paying a number too small to see on screen, and every one of them is repriced. Damage reduction that rounded to nothing went up, rindbound and pressed plate from 5 and 4 to 15. Resists that skipped a tick went up, volcanic from 8 to 40 and stormtouched from 4 to 15. Flat +1 armour on a stat counted in whole points went to 3, and the +2 HP perks went to 8. (nalathnidragon)
[*]The Settled Vein pays the -50% on its card, at every depth. It was a raw point value being run through a curve, so the card and the game disagreed everywhere but tier 1.
[*]Hardened by Hearth doubles, 10 points to 20.
[*]Reserve Marks reroll tradition perks.
[*]Carved Rune and Inkwell's Gift are retired. Inkwell's Gift promised +10% scroll potency and this game has no scrolls.
[/list]

[b]Crafting and building[/b]
[list]
[*]Fourteen recipes no player could reach have real sources. Six mills come from a Sawyer's Guide, the iron and steel hatchet and pickaxe from a Toolsmith's Primer, the War Wheel from its own schematic, and rope, herb mix and premium feed are known from the start.
[*]Each mill knows its species, so milling palm does not spend your oak.
[*]River Shallows and Deep Water fill water back into ground, at 4 wood and 2 stone, and 16 and 4. Deep Water shares the moat glyph, so this is also how a fort takes its moat back. (iso5455, arkhaven)
[*]Adjacent carpets of one dye read as one rug, with the frame only where the rug ends. Different dyes stay different rugs. (datsheeptho)
[*]A rug under furniture keeps its dye and its top row.
[*]Cooking is always an upgrade.
[*]Pack and Clear take reeds and mushrooms, and take the plant rather than its glyph. (bossman62)
[/list]

[b]Settlements and the world[/b]
[list]
[*]The road that arrives at a village meets a street. A village grid has fixed exits and nothing ever laid the spur between them, so 14.3% of crossings were served. Across 298 crossings at three seeds it is now all of them. (bossman62)
[*]Brinehold's lanes are refused the sea the same way its houses are, which is the end of boardwalks to nowhere. (rubberychicken)
[*]The capital builds its fourth cottage. The ground was open and the search was only looking at one band of it. (bossman62)
[*]Notice boards only charge for a cottage they actually built. (bossman62)
[*]A funded cottage finishes as a home rather than a bare box, and the town, the map and the sign all say which cottages you paid for.
[*]Market teleport stones survive a veteran save. The part of the save that records the places you changed was written over the regenerated square cell for cell, portal included. That is the fourth report of this and the first fix measured on a loaded save rather than a fresh world. (rubberychicken)
[*]A warehouse corner door does not seal the room behind it. (datsheeptho)
[*]Wild livestock stop spawning inside pens. (bossman62)
[*]Zone headers name who holds the region you are standing in. (craft16)
[*]Worldforge shores report their own faction owners, and the legend lists them. (craft16)
[*]Territory entry lines wear the faction's colour, and patrol livery follows the shore.
[/list]
