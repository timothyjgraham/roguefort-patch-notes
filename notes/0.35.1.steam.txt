Hey everyone,

This is a hotfix update that irons out some issues with the world map changes in 0.35.0 and does a bit of polishing at the same time to the map.

The big headline for this is that I made a big design decision today. For a long time the world map has had a fog of war, so you had to explore it to reveal the map. While intended to give a sense of completion and revelation of the unknown, at the end of the day it just isn't that great of a design choice. I mulled over this for a few weeks since the launch, and today it is going in, so the full map will be visible.

Rest assured that it only reveals the names and icons for things in the world once you've explored that area. So you still need to explore to places to see what's there (or get a rumour pin if you hear about them), but the world map makes the terrain type clearer, and you get to see the whole continent, which to my eyes looks nicer and doesn't drastically change gameplay.

A few more notes on various fixes below as well as credits for those who reported them - thanks for that.

Thanks for reading and playing,

Tim

[*]Place names (i.e. the text for them on the map) should be much crisper and cleaner at every zoom level and a city's name is bigger than a village's so it stands out better and signifies importance (madness5520)
[*]If two names need the same spot due to the locations being close together on the map, the map hides one of them but it keeps every icon so you still know they are there

[*]Roads stand out on every kind of ground, from snow to desert etc. A road that you have discovered will has a dark outline, and a footpath is a row of grey dots.
