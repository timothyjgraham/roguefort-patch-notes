Hey everyone,

First, thank you. SamV asked for a Minimal setting that really is minimal, and it grew into a whole Comfort page. Most of the rest came straight out of #closed-beta and the forums over the last two days, from darker nights to towns you can walk through without squeezing past fences. Thank you to NalathniDragon, Danek, knifee, Tres and everyone else who reported, tested and talked things through. You'll see your names on almost every line below.

The headlines:

[list]
[*]A new Comfort page in Settings. Flashes, weather on screen, water, background motion, the camera and the interface each have their own levels, and one Sensory-friendly switch turns everything down at once.
[*]Nights follow the moon, darkest at a new moon and brightest at a full one, with a Night brightness slider if you prefer the old bright nights.
[*]Towns are safer and easier to get around. Wild animals stay out unless they're chasing you, and fences and yard clutter keep off the paths.
[*]You can swim, and the lakes near home have pike and eels in them.
[*]Spectre Blue and the other cheeses that let you sense hidden things finally do it.
[*]Rind Cultists cast spells and Ravenkin Slingers throw stones, so the overworld has things that fight from range.
[/list]

All of this comes to every save. Town layouts only change in places you haven't visited yet.

Tim

[b]Comfort and performance[/b]
[list]
[*]Settings has a new Comfort page with its own row for flashes, weather on screen, water, background motion, camera motion and interface motion. Each goes from full down to still, and anything that tells you something keeps a still version instead of disappearing. (samv123)
[*]Sensory-friendly mode is one switch that turns every Comfort row to its quietest and holds visual effects at Minimal. A row it's holding says so. (samv123)
[*]Minimal visual effects calm the whole screen now, with softer flashes, lighter weather, still water, gentler background motion and a still interface. (samv123)
[*]Lightning and other bright flashes never pulse more than three times in just over a second, whatever your settings.
[*]The first time you start a new save, the game asks how you'd like the screen to feel (Full, Calm or Still), and you can change it any time in Comfort. (samv123)
[*]Your first morning in a new game is always storm-free.
[*]Low-power mode in Display caps the game at 30 frames a second and draws less often while you stand still, which helps the battery on a Steam Deck or a laptop.
[*]Behind menus, and when the window isn't in focus, the game drops to about ten frames a second.
[*]A frame rate limit in Display caps the game at 30 or 60, or matches your display.
[/list]

[b]Night and light[/b]
[list]
[*]Nights follow the moon. A new-moon night is properly dark and a full moon lights the ground, while your own light, lit windows, fires and torches still show the way. (palpablebrush26)
[*]Night brightness in Display lifts every night towards the old brightness, and at the top of the slider nights look the way they did before. (palpablebrush26, danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Rooms your Cave Bat maps with its echo show their floors and walls on the main window instead of drawing black. (danekjovaxutc5)
[/list]

[b]Towns and the people in them[/b]
[list]
[*]Wild animals stay out of towns and villages unless they're chasing you. Deer, wolves, boars and bandits don't spawn inside a settlement any more, and a creature that wanders to the edge turns back. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Village tutors run from a wolf, and walk back to their posts once it's gone.
[*]Fences, hedges and stumps don't leave diagonal gaps to squeeze through on your way round town.
[*]Yard clutter keeps towns looking lived in without blocking streets, doors or gates. (samv123)
[*]Every townsperson's first menu has Ask the way. It lists the services that place has, names who runs each one, says which way and how far, and offers to walk you there or mark it on your map. Tutors answer it too. (samv123, .knifee)
[*]Every town has a scholar who identifies any item for 25 gold, smiths look over arms and armour, and a village shopkeeper will have a go as a last resort. The scholar in the Old Mouth identifies items too. (danekjovaxutc5, nalathnidragon)
[*]Shipwrights are on the signposts, and the general store's sign hangs where a newcomer will see it. (danekjovaxutc5, samv123)
[*]A villager coming home in the evening walks in from wherever the day left him, instead of appearing a few tiles from you. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Auto-travel, click-to-walk and auto-explore go round other people's homes. If the place you picked is inside one, you stop at the door. (.knifee, samv123)
[*]Roofs keep their colour as you walk round a building, a door on the far side of a house stays drawn, and roofs vary by building, with thatch or shingles on farms and slate on guildhalls, shrines and inns. (nalathnidragon, aj01657, danekjovaxutc5)
[/list]

[b]Rest and travel[/b]
[list]
[*]Rest and Recover beside a campfire waits until your Manna is full as well as your health, from any side of the fire, and Shift+W recovers Manna in the tutorial. (nalathnidragon, samv123)
[*]Sleeping in a closed-up room doesn't wake you for whatever walks past the window. Anything close, in the room with you, or able to reach you over a wall still wakes you. (.knifee, samv123, danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Holding a movement key stops the moment a new hostile comes into view, and a fresh press walks on. (nalathnidragon)
[/list]

[b]Swimming and the sea[/b]
[list]
[*]You can swim in deep water at half pace. Swim out and Dive in are in the water menu, you can go over a ship's rail or climb back aboard, and there's no drowning.
[*]Each stroke has a one in ten chance of an unlocked item floating out of your pack and drifting ashore. Locked items, keys, books, recipes, maps and story items never float.
[*]Lakes near home have pike and river eels, and eels hide under the water. Bubbles and a ring show where one is, and SURFACES! when it comes up.
[*]Dungeon pools can be swum through.
[*]Galleons and bigger ships hold about three times the hull they did (a galleon is 450 now), and hammerheads, serpents and eels leave a galleon or bigger alone. Your saved boats keep their condition. (danekjovaxutc5, .knifee)
[*]Patch the hull is in the self and helm menus. It mends a tenth of the hull at a time for planks and nails, up to three quarters, and won't work with a hostile nearby. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Dying at sea, or on a ship's deck, wakes you on dry ground at the nearest shore, then a waystone, then home. (.knifee)
[*]Your boat stays steady under you as you sail in first person and 2.5D, instead of jumping a tile each step.
[/list]

[b]Sensing hidden things[/b]
[list]
[*]Spectre Blue, Truffle Camembert, Veilcap Camembert, Wraithpress Blue and Witchcap Stilton let you sense what's hidden within 8 tiles. Secret room doors open, hidden pressure plates show, eels under the water appear, and buried caches get a pin on your map. The Phantom Tongue prize and a grown Crystalline Serpent do the same. (samv123, nalathnidragon)
[*]A Forest Fox's nose finds hidden plates within 4 tiles.
[*]A few effects I'm still finishing, like Keen Eyes, have cards that say plainly what they are without promising more.
[/list]

[b]Combat and magic[/b]
[list]
[*]A Deep-Frozen foe counts as Frozen for every combo. (nalathnidragon)
[*]The Brittle Rind combo's card shows its real +100% and calls the heavy Aged "Aged+". (nalathnidragon)
[*]Combos are numbered in the Codex, so you can see which one fires first when a status could feed two, and the aiming preview only promises the combo that will actually fire. (nalathnidragon)
[*]An enemy's hover card shows its statuses and the turns each has left, and the same card docks on screen for keyboard, controller and first person. (nalathnidragon)
[*]Braziers, burning ground, lava and lingering clouds hit your Rinded ward first, like any other blow. (nalathnidragon)
[*]A spell on your belt glows gold when it would continue a sequence you know, and its card names the sequence. The glow and the log hints can each be turned off in Gameplay. (nalathnidragon)
[*]Right-click a spell recipe in the cheese press, or press the Inspect key, to see the spell it makes. (nalathnidragon)
[*]Heart herbs on the Affineur's bench show their element. (nalathnidragon)
[*]Casting at a rabbit or a hen doesn't train your spells any more. (nalathnidragon)
[*]Roquelle gets the Smoked Gouda Fog recipe in the pack on every start. (samv123)
[*]Upgrades that say "2 turns longer" add exactly two turns on every wheel. On Fine and better wheels that's up to two turns less than before. (nalathnidragon)
[*]A level-up offer never shows two perks that do the same thing. (nalathnidragon)
[*]Rooted Bloom heals 4 a turn out of combat, more than Lattice Bloom and Saturated Bloom. (nalathnidragon, danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Rind Cultists cast now. One marks a red square under you and it goes off two turns later, so two steps clear it. (.knifee, arkhaven)
[*]Ravenkin Slingers throw stones from range on the plains and in the forest by day, from the middle of the map outwards. (.knifee, arkhaven)
[/list]
