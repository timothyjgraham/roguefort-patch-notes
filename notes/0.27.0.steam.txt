Hey everyone,

Thank you to the playtesters and community for testing 0.26.0 and showing me where Fortress was hard to get into. This update makes it much easier to pick up, and brings in a new default font.

Tim

[b]A new font[/b]
[list]
[*]Standard is the new default font. What you read stays in the smooth Readable font, and titles, tabs, buttons and labels use a pixel font.
[*]If you were on Readable, you've been moved to Standard and the game tells you once. To go back, pick Readable in Settings, Display, Font.
[*]Classic and Hyperlegible stay as you set them.
[*]The pixel font is Fusion Pixel by TakWolf.
[/list]

[b]Fortress[/b]
[list]
[*]Starting a colony takes one press. Start your colony is the big button in the middle of the screen, and the world, site and settler options sit under Customise.
[*]If you have a colony saved, the start screen opens on its Resume button, so Enter or A takes you straight back in.
[*]Menu on the top bar, or Escape, opens a Paused menu with Resume, Save colony, Settings, Help and tutorial, Save and exit to title, and Save and quit. Resume carries on at the speed you paused at.
[*]When a save fails, the Paused menu says so in one plain line.
[*]Build has tabs now. It opens on Essentials, which holds everything a new colony needs.
[*]Demolish takes down anything built, cancels anything planned and refunds it, and takes roofs off. Click a tile and Demolish or Cancel order is the first thing offered.
[*]You can plan a whole sleeping room in one drag. The Sleeping room tool lays out the walls, a door, a roof and a bed for every settler, and Room does the same without the beds. Both work around walls that are already standing.
[*]The bed, kitchen and sleeping room lessons hand you the tool and outline the spot on the map. The sleeping room lesson also has Place it for me.
[*]Settlers stay out of the campfire. They gather round it on tiles of their own, and anyone with no work wanders the camp. Older colonies get this too.
[*]Planned things say what they are on their tile card, like Planned stone wall, and a roof waiting on walls you've ordered shows as queued.
[*]The top bar names its buttons when there's room for the words, the bottom bar has Log and More, and the ? button is called Help and tutorial.
[*]The Settlers panel opens on a short summary with the four bars, and the rest folds away underneath.
[*]An empty Gardens page points you to its Build garden button.
[/list]

[b]Keyboard and controller in Fortress[/b]
[list]
[*]Choosing a Build tool with Enter or Space puts you on the map, ready to place it.
[*]When a lesson moves on, the keyboard jumps to its next button.
[*]On a controller, the Paused menu wraps top to bottom like the arrow keys do, and the D-pad reaches every site on the start screen.
[/list]
