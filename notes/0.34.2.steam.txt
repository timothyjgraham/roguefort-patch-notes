Hello Affineurs,

I hope you've had a nice weekend. This is largely a hotfix type of update, with a focus on fixing critical bugs and polishing parts of the game that were either a bit too rough around the edges still, as well as adding one or two features that I want to get a start on.

A heartfelt thanks to everyone who submitted bug reports and making suggestions. I am working hard to get as many as I can done each day, so if yours isn't in here please know that I have it jotted down, and I will be getting to it as soon as I possibly can.

Tim

[b]Fixing issues with loading the game on some machines[/b]
[list]
[*]On some PCs, 0.34.0 played the title music but never opened its window. The window now always opens and the new Interface size is applied once it's up. (jetkhoo, flowkclab, iceblink, Dustlight_, tyloraw, Darkful)
[/list]

[b]Sailing[/b]
[list]
[*]You can now auto travel by boat. While you’re sailing, pressing Shift+X, or using the world map’s “Travel here” or clicking the minimap will all plan a route over water your ship can sail, and then of course take you there
[*]A voyage to a place inland stops at the nearest shore you can you step onto and tells you how far you still have to walk.
[*]On a river, "Travel" tells you to steer your Skiff by hand.
[*]On a ship's deck, Travel takes the helm for you
[*]Boarding a ship no longer sets it moving. It holds its position until you set a heading at the helm wheel, which now offers Hold position and Sail ahead. (bobinhedgeorge, danekjovaxutc5)
[*]A voyage stops if your hull takes a hit and sails past a predator that leaves your ship alone. A co-op passenger is told who has control of the helm.
[*]Big ships no longer get turned back in open sea where a river flows out. A river's size limits now only apply on on the river itself. (Zephyrus)
[*]Help now says the deck autopilot sails at about a third of the hull's speed, whereas before it used to say full speed.
[/list]

[b]Saves[/b]
[list]
[*]Importing a save always makes a new world now. It used to write over your newest one. I'm so sorry about this for anyone it affected. A name that clashes gets "· imported" added. (samv123)
[*]A world you deleted on one machine or a Permadeath character who died no longer comes back from Steam Cloud on your other machine (_ohyou)
[/list]

[b]The Old Mouth and Settings[/b]
[list]
[*]A statue now stands in the Old Mouth for every supporter of the game, in a gallery in the north cave above the plaza. Each one is named on its plaque, on Look and on its hover card. This is the first of a few in-game perk "thank yous" for the people who supported the game on Ko-fi. If you're reading this and you supported the game on itch.io back in the alpha days, please get in touch with me because I'd like to add a monument to you as well, with your permission.
[*]The first time you go into the Old Mouth, the game asks first: Enter, Enter offline, or Not now. (__hariel__, tthfry, enwehr, moonmoon)
[*]New setting, Settings -> Gameplay -> Online -> "Keep the Old Mouth offline". You join no hall, nobody sees you and you see nobody. The Exchange, Wanted board, Post Office and chests say they're closed while offline, and the stalls still sell. (__hariel__, tthfry, enwehr, moonmoon)
[*]New setting, Settings -> Audio -> "Mute in the background". Every sound goes quiet while the game's window is in the background. Off by default. (proteinpauly, naikhou.)
[*]The pause menu shows the game's version. (tthfry)
[/list]

[b]Frame rate[/b]
[list]
[*]At a 30 or 60 frame rate limit, the menus and the HUD now animate on the same beat as the game. Before, they kept the window drawing at your screen's full rate, so Steam's counter read well above the limit. (moleuk, tthfry, shtacks, ghraemdal)
[/list]

[b]Combat[/b]
[list]

[*]In co-op, a player knocked to 0 by a reflected blow is now down and can be revived. They used to stand at 0 HP.
[*]A Rind Cultist's red patch disappears when the cultist dies or stops casting. (jojoknob)
[/list]
