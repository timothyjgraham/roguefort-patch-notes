Hey everyone,

This one is mostly co-op. Danek, Urughak and thesquirrel put a long session through its paces and came back with a list, and most of this update is that list. There are fixes for building, foraging and The Wilds in here too. Thanks everyone.

Tim

[b]Villagers, spells and your standing[/b]
[list]
[*]Walking into a villager, clicking near one or letting your bow pick a target could hit townsfolk who weren't fighting, once a single stray kill had dropped your standing with their faction. Steps, clicks and auto-aim leave them alone now. They tell you why they won't deal with you and you step past. (danekjovaxutc5, urughak)
[*]Guards still fight you in a town that has turned, and you can attack anyone on purpose with right-click and Attack, or with Shift+F.
[*]Aiming a spell names any townsperson standing in its path and marks their tile, so you can see that a Cheddar Shot at a wolf will carry on into the farmer behind it. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]When a spell does hit someone who wasn't fighting, the log says who, with which spell, and that your standing fell for it. The first time it happens in a run you also get a line about the Spare townsfolk rule, which you choose when a run begins.
[*]Under the Self-damage rule the aim line warns you when you're standing in your own blast.
[*]Villagers who won't talk to you have dropped their quest marks, and the hint beside them says hostile where it offered Talk or Trade.
[*]Rumours that printed "blood on {their} hands" say their, his or her as they should.
[/list]

[b]Co-op: quests and joining[/b]
[list]
[*]Guests who pressed Accept on a villager's job could be told "That offer is no longer available" over and over. The host honours the card you were shown now, including after you've thrown a job back, when the giver has wandered a step away, or when time has moved on while you read. (thesquirrel_74982)
[*]If a job really has gone, the giver stops offering it to you.
[*]Joining a friend's older world, you're offered only the jobs that are still open. Givers were putting up Investigate jobs the host had finished long ago.
[*]City crowds show you a quest mark only when they have work for you.
[*]Joining a Permadeath or Hardcore world tells you so as you arrive, and the co-op panel has a Journey line for everyone. If every player falls in one of those worlds, the journey ends for all of you.
[*]Spell warnings follow the host's Spare townsfolk and Self-damage rules for every player. Until now a guest's screen went by the guest's own choice.
[*]Village tutors teach the host for now, and guests are told that when they talk to one. You can still ask a tutor the way and hear what they know. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]A party wipe in a Permadeath world removes that co-op world from a guest's computer and nothing else. In a rare case it could take one of their own saves with it.
[*]Guests get far fewer short freezes while the host's game autosaves. (danekjovaxutc5)
[/list]

[b]Co-op: building, the minimap and giving[/b]
[list]
[*]Guests can walk with a build selected. Arrow keys and the D-pad move you, and Enter, A or a click places the piece. Before this, every step built on the tile ahead and spent the materials.
[*]Guests can move furniture and chests. The Move tool did nothing for anyone but the host. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Friends show on the minimap as a green dot with a white ring, and they sit on its edge when they're out of range. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Standing beside a friend tells you how to give them gold or items, and the co-op panel has a line for it. Giving was already there on right-click, C or L2, with gold as the first row. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]In strict turns the co-op panel sits below the waiting banner, and its labels have room beside their text.
[*]Picking a plant goes by your own Botany, whoever is hosting.
[/list]

[b]Adventure[/b]
[list]
[*]Chests, beds and tables can be built and moved in a room you raised over wild flowers, and that includes rooms you've already built. The flowers under the floor were being counted as something in the way. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Ground you clear, floor or build on stays that way when you load. Flowers were growing back under your floorboards.
[*]Dropped items land on floors laid over flowers.
[*]Dawn Pixies and Moonrings that aren't ready to pick can be walked over, by keys, by clicks, on a long trip or holding Ctrl, and the log tells you when to come back. Out of season they sat there as a wall. (bancheis, danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Thorn Blackberries, Wild Strawberries and every other named berry count as berries in Waymarker's Wrap and Hunter's Roast. Plain Berries are used first and the rarer ones last, and berries you've locked are left alone. (samv123)
[*]Milk restores 15 hunger as well as 15 thirst. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Enemy dots and the Rat King's scout rings have a dark outline on the minimap, so they show up on snow and tundra floors. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Frost Ewes, Moon Hares and Ember Aurochs wait to be found. They bolted at any distance and were gone before you'd seen them. Crouch, creep within five tiles and offer their food. (danekjovaxutc5)
[*]Befriending one legendary beast and then meeting another could interrupt your turn with an error. That's fixed.
[*]Locked doors stay locked while you have a build selected.
[*]When someone has more to say, the prompt under their words reads next where it read close.
[/list]

[b]Survival mode: The Wilds[/b]
[list]
[*]Planting a seed uses it up. Seeds were staying in your pack, which made for a very generous orchard. (palpablebrush26)
[*]Taking a floor down gives back the wood it says it does.
[*]Crops ripen in about three days and a sapling takes about eight to become a tree, sooner in rain. Berries ripening in an afternoon was a bit much. (palpablebrush26)
[*]Planting a tree, and looking at a growing plot, both tell you how long is left.
[*]Oak and the other tree-wood floors and walls can be built, along with Stone and Iron walls. Fourteen pieces on the Build list did nothing when you tried to lay them.
[*]Your run's own days are what the clock in the top bar counts. A new run read Day 63 beside Day 1.
[*]Gill nets catch the fish of the hour they're set in. Every net was pulling in the daytime catch.
[/list]

[b]Known issues[/b]
[list]
[*]Some co-op groups see a delay of a second or more on things the host decides, such as attacks, picking things up and talking, even on a good connection. This update makes the autosave freeze rarer and does not fix that delay. If you see it, a performance report from the host's computer (Ctrl+Shift+P) is the most useful thing you can send.
[/list]
