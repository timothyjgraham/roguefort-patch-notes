Movement, speed and the world clock19

  • Speed buys distance again. A minor road costs 0.500 turns per tile instead of 1.000, so moving fast covers ground rather than making the world act more often.rubberychicken
  • Creatures take exactly one move per player action at every speed. Measured 0.500 moves per action at speed 2 and 0.233 at speed 4 before, 1.000 at both after.rubberychicken
  • Sixteen world systems that ran at speed-times their proper rate on a road now run at world pace: weather, creature spawns, spell cooldowns, cheese aging, hunger and thirst, ecology, NPC family life, hostility decay, youth passing, aura damage, flora regrowth, companion ability ticks, ambient night, terroir biome, death ticks and the companion tick. Roads no longer make you twice as hungry or roll twice as many spawns per turn of world time.rubberychicken
  • Sneak XP, granted every 50th turn, was unobtainable while on a road because the turn counter's parity collapsed to a single residue at speed 2 and 4. It is reachable at every speed.
  • The companion abilities Web Trap and Silk Snare advertise a 1-turn immobilise and cost the target 0.00 creature turns on a road. Both now cost a full creature turn on any surface.rubberychicken
  • Trap and status deadlines (immobilise, stall, invisibility, poison, mount skittishness, wards, summons, theft cooldown, dodge momentum) last the same number of world turns on a road as off it, and keep their remaining duration through a save and reload.
  • Long-distance travel over roads saves daylight instead of costing it. Routes costing more daylight than beelining went from 20 of 20 to 0 of 33, mean daylight change from +31.1% to about -21%. Routes remain about 16% longer in tiles.rubberychicken
  • The travel router priced distance as the crow flies while the player walks an eight-connected grid, over-charging the beeline by up to 40% depending on which direction the destination lay. It now uses the walked metric.
  • The long-route planner prices road classes separately, so a curated highway is no longer costed the same as a village footpath. The local 50-tile pathfinder gained a major-road tier (wilderness 6, minor road and trail 3, major road 2), and the trail cost was corrected.
  • Autosave is denominated in actions rather than world turns and anchors on the first action it observes. Autosaves were being stepped over entirely on a road, and a fresh game no longer saves immediately.
  • Four cheeses (Glacier Wheel, Goldhour Tomme, Pistachio Curd, Goldhour Grand) and the travel treatise grant +1 speed that reaches movement. The bonus had been folded into a stat nothing read.rubberychicken
  • Around thirty existing strings became true with no edit: road and tile labels, the four "You pick up speed on the ..." messages, the bat-form claims, every mount description, the three terroir speed perks and Road Runner.
  • Movement animation is four separate settings under Settings, Display, Movement (2D): You, Enemies, Townsfolk and Wildlife, Companions and Workers, each set to Hop, Slide or Instant. The shipped default is you glide and the rest of the world hops. Saves that had already chosen a style keep it on all four categories.
  • Every mover arcs about 0.18 tile over its 100ms step and lands with a 90ms squash. ASCII mode gets the arc without glyph scaling; the ground shadow stays down.
  • Wild livestock is classified as wildlife rather than as a companion, and townsfolk who turn hostile move on the enemy setting.
  • Riding follows your own movement setting, where the mount's gallop bound was previously applied to every mounted step regardless.
  • Dismounted mounts, led pack animals, hired workers and following or wandering livestock now animate when they move. All of them previously teleported one tile at a time.
  • Walking in 2D scrolled the world for 100ms and then stood still. The glide was hard-coded at 100ms against a measured step cadence of 141ms, so the camera was frozen for 26% of frames drawn while stepping. Glide duration now tracks a moving average of the measured cadence, clamped 90 to 220ms, and goes linear while movement is continuous. Dead time per step is 0ms and frozen frames 1%.theartolater
  • The 3D and first-person renderers had the same defect on a fixed 110ms glide. Dead time per step went from 34.8ms to 0.

Combat28

  • Every player strike lands contact in the first sixth of the swing. Time to contact: slash 244ms to 64, overhead 336 to 90, stab 150 to 44, thrust 249 to 69, punch 146 to 40, mine 276 to 59, chop 244 to 60, gather 209 to 50, cast 335 to 110, bow 326 to 135. At least half of each timeline is now recovery, and pull-back distance dropped from 0.16 to 0.06 tiles. Enemy creatures keep their slower wind-up, because their wind-up is what warns you an attack is coming.
  • Landing a hit briefly freezes the game: about 45ms on a melee hit, 45ms on impact, 65ms on a kill, 75ms on a crystal burst. Struck sprites flash white for about two frames in 2D as well as 3D.
  • The anticipation crouch, strike-peak stretch and heavy impact dip are applied in 2D, where only the 3D renderer used them. The camera nudges about 2px into the attack on contact and springs back over 180ms.
  • Holding the harvest key shows one visible strike per 120ms tick, so held mining and chopping reads as rhythmic hammering.
  • Three melee stance passives: Cleave (dual wield, 25% of the swing to up to 3 hostiles adjacent to both you and your target), Sweep (two-handed, 20% to up to 4 other hostiles at arm's length) and Shield Bash (weapon and shield, the shield's own armor value clamped to 1-3 when an enemy misses you). Splash is measured off pre-sneak damage, respects the same targeting rules as your bump attack, and the inventory stance chip names your active passive. First pass numbers, expected to be retuned.onixdarkem8595
  • A polearm family of four two-handed reach weapons: boar spear (2-3 damage plus armor, area level 1), glaive (2-4 plus crit, level 3), halberd (3-4, level 4), pike (3-5, level 5). Sold by blacksmiths and merchants, dropped from chests, dungeons and boss pools. They borrow the spear sprite until their art is painted.urughak
  • Ranged attacks could only hit a multi-tile creature's top-left corner, so shooting a 4x4 dragon's flank answered "No target there!". Range, visibility and line of sight are asked of the nearest occupied cell.sarcen
  • A hostile standing next to you sees you and attacks. The sneak detection roll had no floor at range 1, so a crouched, geared player at night could cancel the adjacency term outright and an awake hostile would roll and fail forever. Detection is automatic at distance 1 for awake creatures; sleeping creatures stay exempt.
  • A blinded or smoked creature swings again at arm's reach. An adjacent blinded pursuer had been doing zero damage over six turns.
  • Creatures can only make a ranged attack from a tile the player can currently see, and a refused shot falls through to closing the distance instead of the archer freezing. Melee, senses, stealth, pursuit, dragon breath, caster telegraphs and companion-targeted shots are exempt.rubberychicken
  • Missed and dodged bow shots alert the target. The alert was written and then discarded by the same turn's roster publish.
  • Damage-over-time ticks, landmines and companion splash now wake sleeping creatures and record who hit them, so a sleeper can no longer be burned down without waking. A chained hit that kills and grants a free turn also wakes a struck sleeper.rubberychicken
  • Floating damage numbers showed the pre-mitigation figure while the log showed the real one. All 16 damage sources plus zone and burst ticks float the post-mitigation number, and a blow refused by Old Mouth peace, absorbed by a shield or aimed at a dead creature floats nothing.rubberychicken
  • A death from a damage-over-time tick or a zone counts as a kill: shatter, cascade, death-spread and affinage kill triggers all fire, keyed off the killing status's source spell. Zone kills previously ran no on-death path at all. This raises rot build power.
  • The base plague-burst death spread on a DOT kill was cosmetic, printing "+Curdled" over creatures that never received it. Measured 0 of 3 ring creatures infected versus 1 of 3 on a direct kill.
  • Twin Flame, Double Wheel and Twin Spikes never fired on an area spell, because the second strike picked six nearest foes and only then discarded the ones already hit. The exclusion is applied before the cap.
  • Shatter Shard and Death Spread each tested one status where their wording names a family, so five picks could never fire. Frost and rot are now each named once as a family.
  • Paradox Burst's guaranteed Shatter synergy was dropped as a duplicate where the combo would have happened anyway and rejected where it was needed, against the 13 creature kinds immune to fire or ice. It fires on those targets, once per target per cast.
  • Arc Strike could not chain to an adjacent enemy at or above 999 HP because the lowest-HP picker was seeded with the literal 999. It works against the 640 HP roaming boss past endless-night scaling and against endgame 1200 HP targets.
  • Grand Affineur's Ward lapses after one full day-night cycle instead of being a permanent damage pool feeding ward armor into your stats. It also shields active companions within its ally radius; companions previously had no absorb field at all. Existing saves keep their rind and get a fresh day on load.
  • All spells can hit a ghost phased into a wall. BEAM spells include the wall tile they stop at, CONE spells admit first-surface wall tiles, and BURST still has to be centred on the ghost's own tile. Auto-snap targeting, the cast preview and the AOE highlight all show them. Melee and ranged shots still pass through.arkhaven
  • The spell targeting overlay under-drew its own blast: the BOLT+ shatter and the "explodes on impact" burst both add a square the preview never showed, so Avalanche Wheel drew one tile and damaged nine. No damage value changed.theartolater
  • The HUD attack chip shows the real swing total including fire, cold and poison, with an itemised hover. The Character panel's Fire, Cold and Poison Damage rows render at all, having previously read stat keys that do not exist.
  • Ten spell upgrades printed a raw internal token at the player instead of readable text. All ten now take their wording from their own card text.
  • Status effects consolidated from 16 to 11, each with one meaning: BURNING merged into BLISTERED, CRYSTALLIZED into FROZEN (stun 2, no slow), CONCUSSED into SMOKED (-6, and it blinds), BRITTLE into AGED, MAGGOTED into CURDLED. DEEP_FROZEN stays as the named residue exception. Existing saves keep their statuses through the merge.NalathniDragon
  • A status with no definition used to last forever; unknown status ids count down and expire. Re-applying a status can no longer weaken a live one, and anything immune to BLISTERED blocks tile fire.
  • Poison and sickness stay two separate statuses with two separate resist wires.
  • A vampire player entering a settlement with a popup open threw an error that the turn handler swallowed, skipping the rest of that turn including weather, spawns, vitals and the companion tick.

Spell combos, sequences and chains17

  • FLASH_FREEZE, BRITTLE_RIND and AGED_CRYSTAL fired a toast, a Codex unlock and a narrator line and changed nothing. They leave real residue: FLASH_FREEZE consumes BRINED and FROZEN for DEEP_FROZEN (3-turn stun, slow after, 1.5x damage taken, 6 turns), BRITTLE_RIND consumes FROZEN and AGED for BRITTLE (2.0x damage taken, 4 turns), AGED_CRYSTAL consumes CRYSTALLIZED and AGED for BRITTLE at 6 turns.nalathnidragon
  • Every synergy states its mechanical effect alongside its flavour text, in the Codex row and in the narrator message, derived from the effect itself so the text cannot disagree with the engine.
  • Applying a residue or spread status can complete another combo in the same cast, marked with a chain prefix, bounded by a per-cast duplicate check, a depth cap of 3 and a queue cap of 64.
  • One ingredient status can no longer fuel two combos. Both previously fired off stale snapshots and could resurrect a consumed status.
  • Combo damage scales at +5% per level, capped at 3x, applied to synergy AoE damage, zone tick damage and the bonus part of on-hit multipliers. Base spell damage stays flat.
  • FERMENTED_FIRE and PUTRID_INFERNO never reached the synergy executor, so their amp persisted for the status's whole duration while the Codex claimed one ignition. They consume their ingredient and fire once per target per status window.
  • Synergy recipes cut from 18 to 12 after the status merge made three of them exact duplicates of rules that already consumed the same ingredients.
  • Spell sequences: casting one family and then another within 3 turns rides an extra effect on the second cast. Eight curated, deliberately asymmetric pairs. One opener fuels one sequence and is consumed; the completing cast is itself a fresh opener, so rhythms chain (HEAT to COLD to ROT is Quench then Cold Rot). Most riders apply ingredient statuses that can immediately trigger a full combo.
  • Casting the opener of a discovered sequence prints a telegraph, throttled to once per 20 turns. The Codex gained a Spell Sequences section with recipe and derived mechanics lines, discovery is shared in co-op, and each sequence has its own VFX and SFX.
  • Grand Sequences: three-cast combos within a 4-turn window. Fondue Course (HEAT, FORCE, COLD) fires a full-strength chain; Affineur's Round (ROT, ARCANE, CONTROL) applies FERMENTED in radius 2 and arms a ROT sequence. A grand match is preferred over a two-cast match and consumes both openers.
  • BOLT, BEAM and LOB spells carrying a chain spec hit additional targets as full hits at a compounding falloff per hop, using the same hop-picker ARC spells always had. Each hop plays its impact effect on a 110ms beat with a mounting counter. The outcome is decided immediately and the animation plays it back afterwards.
  • New affinage branch, FILATA the Stretching Room, with four arts: Pull the Curd (BOLT spells chain once at 50%), Long Pull (+1 hop and a 0.75 falloff floor), Culture Line (each hop applies BRINE for 2 turns) and Unbroken String (a kill mid-chain extends the chain).
  • Three new shrine grafts: Press Wiredraw (FORCE, any bolt, beam or lob chains once at 75%), Kiln Fondue Strand (HEAT, chains once and each hop applies MELTED for 2 turns), Cave Culture Relay (ROT, a kill spreads CURDLED to 2 foes). Shrine mutator ceiling raised from 3 to 4.
  • Two new spells, taking the roster from 52 to 54 and recipes from 43 to 45: Fondue Wire (HEAT tier 2 ARC, applies MELTED) and Aged Filament (ARCANE tier 3 ARC, applies AGED at deliberately low damage as a setup wire). Both have full three-tier mastery tables and recipes on existing family flora. Art pending.
  • A fifth charge type, Curd-Line, minted on every chain hop and spent only on a cast that can use a hop, so a Ward never eats one. Existing saves get it backfilled.
  • Spell descriptions cover chains: a chaining spell reads "chains to N foes at X% per hop", a stamped trigger reads "On each chain hop: ...", and the composition panel adds chain grants cumulatively so Long Pull is never absent and Pull the Curd no longer overstates its solo falloff.
  • The Codex gained Grand Sequences and a derived Chains section, plus a reach line answering whether your loadout can walk a sequence.

  • The attunement choosing phase shows a ledger of upgrades you already own in that discipline, plus a Shift+Y pointer. Only the perk phase had a build rail before.urughak

  • The help screen no longer claims terroir branches lock at your second pick. Branches never lock, keystones open at four picks in a branch, and picks are permanent.rubberychicken

Any closed path of walls, windows and doors up to 400 tiles shelters everything inside it, including bare grass floor and farm plots. Performance13

  • The AFK freeze is fixed. Going away and returning, or loading a save, left the window black with no audio while the game kept accepting input. Three causes, all in the frame loop: the loop scheduled its next frame only after finishing its whole body, so a single error anywhere in that body stopped it for good; the 3D renderer did not recover when macOS dropped its graphics context on an idle window; and the elapsed-time value was unclamped, so the first frame back after an hour handed a 3600-second step to about 15 subsystems at once. The loop schedules first, the renderer recovers its context, and the time step is capped at a third of a second.
  • Generation-time tree health was counted as player damage, making it 77% of the save file. On one real 8.6 MB save, 761,112 health entries of which 760,871 were untouched generated trees against 241 entries of real player damage. The save drops to 1.96 MB, health data from 6,374 KB to 31 KB, and compression from 992ms to 173ms, with a one-time drain that cleans existing saves.
  • The same mistake was evicting live chunks. Nearly every forested chunk read as modified, so the first eviction pass never found a victim and the fallback pass could evict the chunk you were standing in, which the next turn regenerated. Measured 984 chunk generations for 291 distinct chunks over 2,000 turns of walking straight.
  • The engine's cheap idle frame tiers run again. The "is anything animating?" test included a condition true for every default player, so a motionless player paid a full 2,100-cell rebuild thirty times a second.
  • The post-processor uploads only the changed region of the 2D canvas each frame instead of the whole thing, and the per-cell colour calculation is cached, saving 2.91ms per frame over 2,100 cells.theartolaterunknownuluguru_
  • 3D and 2.5D: the terrain heightfield is cached, idle point lights switch all-on or all-off so fading torches and lightning stop recompiling every lit surface, the wall geometry cache cap went from 192 to 1024 so settlements stop disposing and rebuilding every wall each frame, and the 2,200-star field is not submitted at noon. Chunk generation 6.26 to 4.82 ms, 2.5D scene rebuild 13.9 to 10.8ms, first-person rebuild 16.4 to 13.1ms, shader programs 49 to 32, visible scene objects 2,985 to 1,696.
  • Biome terrain generation only evaluates its eight biome-specific noise calls when they are needed instead of running twelve layers of noise per tile before the biome switch.
  • The ambient weather-particle spawn accumulator ran away. It drained only inside the particle-cap check, so once the cap was reached every tick spawned a replacement at frame rate instead of every 400ms. It drains whether or not anything spawns and banks at most one interval of credit.
  • Background saves are floored at 30 seconds apart and deferred to idle time. A coalesced re-run restarted immediately, so a capture showed three saves starting inside 41 seconds while each took about 11.5s, leaving a save in flight about 85% of the time.theartolater
  • The performance-hiccup advisory fired on alt-tabs and stayed silent through real stutter.   • The Ctrl+Shift+P performance report carries motion-continuity figures (dead ms per step, step interval, glide duration, steps fully covered, frozen frame share, longest no-frame gap) using the same definitions the development performance checks use, plus perf counters, snapshot, memory, long-task and advisory fields.
  • The save-time block measurement was taken before the save writer ran, so serialisation, the worker clone and two multi-megabyte writes were invisible, reporting 20ms of blocking against an 11.5s total. It is split into build and write halves.
  • A janky frame no longer credits its whole stall to the hit-stop clock, which was yanking time backwards and visibly restarting a swing in progress. Per-frame freeze credit is capped at 50ms.

Saves and stability10

  • Fog of war stores each chunk's explored area as its exact per-tile shape (8x8 block-sparse packing, 17 bytes for a fully explored chunk) instead of rounding up to whole 16-tile cells or whole chunks. The chunk-eviction trim packs overflow into an uncapped archive instead of deleting it, and the degrade path that baked false "fully explored" data into the save on re-entry is gone.theartolater
  • Saves written before 22 August carry no shape data, so each shapeless chunk is judged once on load: one whose eight neighbours were all entered stays revealed, and a frontier chunk reads as unexplored until walked again. On one reporter's 4,591-chunk save this re-clouds 1,421 chunks and stops 5.8M tiles being shown as explored. Raw map JSON went from 1,441 KB to 1,120 KB, and compressed saves grew about 5.4% because exact shapes no longer compress away as zero runs.
  • A deep dungeon run is no longer forgotten because you walked out of it past inns, caves and cellars. Every interior shared one cache capped at five entries by deleting the oldest, so stepping into an inn to heal burned a floor off the dungeon you were running. Eviction now sheds the interior with the fewest cached floors first, after the dungeon you are standing in. Measured: a five-floor run's floors 1-3 were deleted and floor 1 returned with 116 explored tiles and no DOWN button; all five are kept and floor 1 returns with all 14,641 tiles.theartolater
  • A save made while aboard a boat was treated as stranded on load and relocated to your home base, at 400 tiles offshore as readily as 1000. A save with the player legitimately afloat is exempt.rubberychicken
  • A save written before fog of war, in February 2026, white-screened on load. Its one-time migration threw during render with no catch on the path, and the error boundary killed the load.
  • The daily settlement reputation ledger is written into the save payload, where it would have reset on every save and load.
  • Changing the season no longer snaps back on the next keypress; the change was writing an absolute turn value the clock could not be lowered to.
  • Opening Mail from a roster row lands on the Send tab, which renders your whole backpack, and the first attachment row threw a reference error, so it crashed for any player carrying an item.arkhaven
  • "You have mail" announced into an empty mailbox while a mailbox holding real unread letters announced nothing. The two halves read different stores, one counting actual letters and the other summarising replayed rows that also include your own vault takes, chest donations, listing posts and bids.theartolatercorroborated by arkhaven
  • Weapon swap could lose or duplicate a stowed weapon, because a stale copy of the pack could be written back over the swap. Fixed, along with the same class of bug in the ammo path.arkhaven

Known issues and deliberate calls17

  • Glacier Spike deterministically misses at range 2 while Cinder Bolt and Avalanche Wheel hit from the same tile at the same target.
  • Squares of falsely explored map already baked into an existing save are indistinguishable from real walked ground and stay. 482 kept-whole chunks border a re-clouded one, so a straight chunk edge can survive inside old explored regions.
  • The retained-floor budget is a flat count across all interiors, so a player nine or more floors into a twelve-floor dungeon can still lose the shallowest ones. If this bites you mid-run, please raise it with us in the support channel.
  • At minimap zoom 2 a long dungeon name still runs the header under the minimap.
  • Provenance chips do not yet appear on other players' auction listings. Rare and above items are still refused from listings, so the new minimum listing price has no effect yet.
  • A joiner's befriend row shows generic label copy for cub, mythic and stag targets. The host's routed refusal names the true requirement on any live click. Players on an older build ignore the new perk-recast action without showing a message.
  • The spell AOE preview still does not draw two things a blast does: ignites setting flammable ground alight and spreading, and blast survivors carrying BURNING wherever they walk.
  • Damage from environmental hazards, damage over time and starvation writes HP directly and ignores temp HP, because absorption is wired into the three creature-attack paths only.
  • Whether holding two copies of the free-turn perk should bank two tokens is still undecided; it stays capped at one for now.
  • Undoing tradition perks with Reserve Marks is not implemented. Treasure maps carry no known-marker.
  • Worlds created before the housing generation flag existed get no notice board anywhere, measured at 1 of 267 settlements.
  • Settlements already visited in an existing save keep their empty inns, their old street layouts and their already-generated decorative flowers.
  • Melee stance, tea, floor cap, patronage and auction numbers are a first pass and are expected to be retuned after playtest.
  • Corvax's pace and the ship autopilot were deliberately left on the old speed model for this release. The HUD pill reading full boat speed during autopilot is unreconciled.
  • Scenario turn limits were not retuned: The Ambassador stays at 8,000 world turns and The Pursuit at 3,000, which at speed 2 is 16,000 and 6,000 keypresses.
  • The 25% execute threshold is unreachable on eleven 1-3 HP creatures, all of which die to any single hit. All 17 bosses are unaffected.
  • The shadow wolf remains unreachable, its only spawn tables being homeland-only bands.
