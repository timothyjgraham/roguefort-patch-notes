Hey everyone,

This is the biggest update the playtest has had. 0.22.0 went out without patch notes, so these cover both builds: everything since 0.21.0.

The big additions are Fortress, a colony mode on the title screen, and the affineur's bench, where you design your own spell cheese. The Steam Deck and controllers got a long pass, the world looks very different in all three views, and dungeons have secret rooms and sieges. After that comes a long list of fixes, most of them from your reports, with your names on them.

Thanks to everyone who sent saves, screenshots and reports. Enjoy.

Tim

Fortress11

  • Fortress is a new mode on the title screen. Generate a continent, pick a starting site, name your colony and found it with seven settlers.
  • You don't play one character in Fortress. You pause, set the speed and drag orders across the map for digging, felling, building and drawing water, and your settlers take the work, haul it to storage, eat, sleep and slowly get better at their jobs.
  • Colonies start with a surface, a cellar and deep rock with caves hidden in it. Farm wheat and cave truffles, milk goats and a cow, and age cheese on racks, where a wheel aged underground yields an extra one.
  • Seasons, weather, fire, flooding and roof collapse all happen to your colony. Settlers get hurt, make friends and rivals, and visitors ask to join, up to 32 living settlers. Salt caravans come to trade.
  • Raiders come too, and they fight and hit exactly as they do in Adventure. Craft spears, slings, mail and shot, and each raid arrives as a letter with Locate and Defense buttons.
  • Send expeditions across the world chart, set up outposts, chase ambitions, and retire a colony when you're done with it.
  • A guided first day walks you through your people, a first tree order, beds, food and water. You can skip it or tuck it away.
  • Fortress has its own soundtrack, 17 tracks by alkakrab, and combat music fades in when raiders arrive.
  • WASD and the arrow keys pan the camera, Space always pauses, and every colony key can be rebound.rubberychickentrespiratesque
  • Whole colonies were dying of thirst beside a well. Settlers now drink water left on the ground, caregivers carry water to settlers who can't walk, and wells keep drawing.trespiratesque
  • Starting sites cover every biome and far fewer sit on mountains, and about half of new settlers roll a good trait.trespiratesque

The affineur's bench12

  • Pressing a cheese in the full game opens the affineur's bench, where you design your own spell cheese. Bring curd and two heart herbs, pick a working to give the spell its shape, choose how long to age it, and press. The named recipes you already know sit behind Known cheeses.
  • There are eight attack workings and four friendly ones. The 25 heart herbs are plants you already gather, and which of them a press will take depends on the country it stands in.
  • Your wheel is named for its region, herb and age, something like Meadow Heather Wheel. It stays on your spell belt for good, and a cast costs Manna and a cooldown, never the cheese.
  • A panel shows what each ingredient brings before anything is spent. Use up three of one herb and you learn its rind working, an extra effect your wheels can carry.arkhaven
  • Put a crafted wheel on a cheese cave's ageing rack and it ages by the calendar, even while you're away. At seven days it gains a second rind working and its region's effect, at forty days a third, and a wheel left for a year or two picks up the long-lay effects.
  • Friendly wheels heal, give Rinded or clear poison, sickness, slowing and corrosion. They reach you, co-op partners, pets, hirelings and summons, and can never heal an enemy.
  • Emmental Mend is a new Swiss wheel that heals you and every wounded friend within two tiles.arkhaven
  • On a full belt the game asks which slot a new wheel should take, and spells can be dragged between belt slots. Whether picked-up war cheese goes straight onto the belt is a setting, on by default.theartolaterbancheis
  • Crafted wheels stack with the same wheel bought or found, and packs that had split them fold back together.danekjovaxutc5
  • Padlocks guard your cheese at the press, the germinator and the Reserve Cellar as well.trespiratesquearkhaven
  • The Recipe Book leads each spell cheese with the spell it makes, ticks or crosses every ingredient, and has a search box.rubberychicken.knifee
  • Forgefloor Cheddar and the other forge cheeses give 25% burn resistance. Their fire resistance had never done anything.rubberychicken

Steam Deck and controllers9

  • The game recognises a Steam Deck more reliably, and Gaming Mode always opens fullscreen and sharp.tthfry
  • Text fields typed every key twice in Gaming Mode. That's fixed, and on a pad every text field opens the game's own on-screen keyboard.tthfry
  • Putting the Deck to sleep saves your game, and the sound comes back when it wakes.
  • Joining a friend from the Steam friends list works when Roguefort was closed.peacook
  • Every action can be reached by pad, and prompts across the game show pad buttons. Javelins, bows and polearms aim by pad, and so do scouts.tthfry
  • Tap R1 for the Panels hub and hold it to swap weapons.
  • The Post Office, the Exchange and trading all work by pad.
  • With a docked Deck and a second controller, the game follows whichever one you press.tthfry
  • Walking in 2.5D runs at about twice the frame rate on a slow machine, and the regular hitch from far mountains is gone.tthfry

How the world looks11

  • Walls, floors and ground are redrawn everywhere, in every dungeon biome, town, interior and ship. Ancient Ruins, the Underground Grove, the Sunken Library and the Old Mouth have been dressed by hand.
  • Grass is darker and greener, roads have their dithered edges back, and the desert shows its dunes again.danekjovaxutc5
  • You can build 63 new pieces of furniture, from tables, beds and bookcases to banners and a scarecrow. Chests store things, beds let you rest and the lamp gives light.
  • Shadows follow the sun, the moon and the weather in all three views, and Settings has a Shadows checkbox.
  • Water in 2D is alive, with sun glints, surf running up the beach, rolling swell and rings where you wade and where rain lands. In 2.5D and first person the sea surfs, lakes lap, rivers run and marshes bubble.
  • Clouds cast their own shadows, storms are dark and heavy, and lightning lights up the scene.
  • Days have a golden hour, a blue hour after sunset and a rose dawn, and at night campfires, torches and lamps throw warm pools of light.
  • Fireflies come out on warm nights in 2.5D and first person, smoke rises from lived-in houses, and mist lies on the water on clear mornings.
  • Levelling up is a moment now. A rune seal stamps out under your feet, a column of light goes up and a shield with your new level rises.
  • First person was rebuilt with a pixel sky, mouse look, your own arms in your gear, roofs on houses, and hills and villages on the horizon. Tab (Y on a pad) opens its Actions menu.
  • Menus and windows have a new look, with navy panels, teal buttons and one pixel frame round every window.

Co-op9

  • Rejoin a world and you keep your name, guild, level, perks and pack. On Steam a friend is matched by their account.rubberychicken
  • Walking on a long-lived save used to lag the whole party, with ping climbing to a full second on every step. A quiet step sends a few bytes now.rubberychicken
  • Each player has their own Arts and Spell Mastery.
  • Turn bubbles reach 12 tiles by default, and the host can set anything from 6 tiles to Everywhere.morighshexara
  • Your turn alert shows over open menus, plays a sound and blinks the window title.morighshexara
  • When a join fails, a screen tells you why: different versions, different world settings or a full party.
  • Save party gives every player a copy they can play.
  • Two players using one chest, barrel or bench at the same moment can't duplicate or lose items, and Exchange listings and Post Office letters only ever pay out once.rubberychicken
  • Partner health reads against their real maximum.rubberychicken

Dungeons10

  • About a third of dungeon floors hide a secret room. Stand beside its door for a few turns and the wall gives way. What waits inside suits the dungeon, and the chest rolls Rare or better.Splattercat
  • A few plains and forest dungeon mouths are beset holdfasts, walled yards under siege. Five waves come over the wall, and the strongbox stays barred until the last one is down.
  • Every dungeon has a companion worth freeing somewhere in its cages. Three dungeons in four had nobody to rescue.danekjovaxutc5
  • Cages hold more than rats, and an empty-looking cage may hold one of the floor's own monsters, which comes out fighting.drachasortrespiratesquedatsheeptho
  • Arctic, taiga, snow peak, glade, savanna and coastal dungeons have their own layouts, monsters and bosses. More than half of dungeons used to end with the Archlich. The plains boss is now The Stilled Affineur.danekjovaxutc5arkhaven
  • Clear a dungeon and a tougher band may move in at a later season.
  • Enemies that lost you behind a door used to walk into the wall and vanish. They stay on the floor now.rubberychicken
  • Your Cave Bat's echolocation works and maps nearby rooms while it follows you.danekjovaxutc5
  • Searched containers say so, and dungeon chests never offer a blueprint you already know.danekjovaxutc5nalathnidragon
  • Your mines keep paying while you're in the Old Mouth or down the Descent.rubberychicken

Combat and weapons8

  • F aims whatever you're holding. A sword strikes beside you, a spear reaches any tile two out, and a bow or javelin aims at range. Step toward an enemy two tiles away with a spear and you swing at it.rubberychickennalathnidragon
  • Javelins are a new one-handed weapon. They stack to five, you pick them back up after a throw, and a following dog fetches them.danekjovaxutc5
  • Cursed gear carries a bargain now: strong stats with a nasty catch, shown on its card. Unidentified gear waits in your pack until you know what it is.danekjovaxutc5
  • A new run rule lets your own blasts catch you. It's off unless you choose it.danekjovaxutc5
  • While hidden you can walk up to a sleeping creature and backstab it, and calm monsters leave you alone..knifeedanekjovaxutc5
  • Each banked level-up deals its own three cards. No more taking the same mythic twenty times.tobacoosdrachasor
  • Vampires reach for Fresh Blood when hungry, and dawn is called at the right time.rubberychicken
  • New characters start on full health.

Known issues5

  • Only the Monadic Descent is open. The Dyadic Vault, the Triadic Gate and the Tetradic Keep are still sealed.
  • Fortress is mouse and keyboard only for now, and it only draws in 2D.
  • In Fortress, a roof built beside a support can still collapse.trespiratesque
  • The Deck changes were tested on a desktop set up like a Deck. On a Deck, use the Linux build: the Windows build run through Proton doesn't see a controller.
  • Crafted spell cheese balance isn't settled, and How to Play doesn't cover the affineur's bench yet.
