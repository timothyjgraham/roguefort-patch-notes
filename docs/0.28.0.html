Hey everyone,

Thanks again to the playtest team for testing 0.27.0 and for all the reports, videos and saves. This is a big one (when is it not in this game?). Everyone in the world has a new, more detailed look, and you can finally choose your hair. Fortress has been rebuilt for Early Access, so a new colony starts somewhere with water and the colony goals walk you from your first well through to Frost and beyond. There's also a long list of fixes from your reports.

Tim

New characters4

  • Everyone is drawn with the new detailed characters, in the world, the inventory, portraits and dialogue. If you'd rather have the original sprites, turn off Detailed characters in Settings, Gameplay, Character and HUD.
  • Sleepers lie on their beds under a blanket with their head on the pillow, and bedrolls lie flat in 2.5D and first person.
  • People sit in chairs, tread water and stroke when they swim, face the float when they fish and kneel at a lock they're picking.
  • The figures on the title menu are the new characters too. The New Game traveller walks east and every few strides jogs, sneaks, hops, forages or turns to greet you.

Character creation6

  • The creation screen puts your character first. There are 24 hairstyles, 16 hair colours and a mixer for any colour you like, and Roll a look picks one for you.
  • The HAIR FROM row jumps between each origin's styles, so any style is only a few presses away.
  • Your character breathes, turns all the way round, shows off their origin's move with their own weapon and does a little hop.
  • Your starting kit is shown as pictures. Hover over one, or move to it on a controller, to see what it is.
  • Show headgear sits beside the head slot in your inventory, or press Shift+M, so you can show off your hair without taking your hat off. A hidden hat still gives its stats, and co-op partners see what you see.
  • Hairstyles show with Detailed characters off too.

Fortress7

  • Fortress has been rebuilt for Early Access. Most of this reaches colonies you begin with Start your colony in 0.28.0. A colony from an older save keeps its old rules and its guided tutorial.
  • Start your colony picks a site with lasting fresh water, or rock to build a well from, so a new colony doesn't die of thirst in its first week.
  • You begin with three settlers, and the founding screen still offers a larger party of seven.
  • Your party arrives with packed supplies instead of ready-made buildings, and the game starts paused so you can look around and plan.
  • A party bound for a dry or rocky site packs extra stone, and the founding screen warns you when the only water nearby is a small pond that will run dry.
  • The step-by-step tutorial makes way for short First evening help and the colony goals.danekjovaxutc5rubberychicken
  • Coal and copper seams lie close to every new camp, and Prospect, in Plan's Orders tab or on Shift+P, shows every seam within 5 tiles.

Fortress goals and alerts9

  • Colony goals walk you from fresh water to a hot meal, a harvest, beds, a roof, food for Frost, the herd, digging, ore, smelting and arms. Each goal has a button that opens the right tool, and Hide goals and hints puts them away.
  • Goals point at the next real step. They tell you the wheat is growing and when it will ripen, ask for timber or stone when a plan is waiting on it, ask you to Make room to store goods when the stores fill up, and find a newcomer a bed in the sleeping room you already have.
  • "Frost is coming" shows three days ahead when Frost would hurt the colony, and an animal in the cold gets its own alert, like "Clover is freezing".
  • A new alert names any settler cut off from the colony's food or water, and Go to it takes you to them.
  • Low water warnings lead you straight to the Well.
  • Go to it on a dig job lands on the right level after a reload, storage alerts name the good with nowhere to go, and one trance brings one notice.trespiratesque
  • Inviting a traveller clears their arrival alert.trespiratesque
  • The pause sound plays when the colony pauses itself for an alert.trespiratesque
  • Read on a raid letter opens the letter, and Locate finds the raider.

Fortress water, food and Frost7

  • A thirsty settler drinks at a nearby fresh bank, the same as the goat does.
  • A garden keeps growing wheat after every harvest until you pause it.
  • Ripe wheat waits out a heat haze for a good while before it withers.
  • A garden out of seed tells you that caravans sell wheat seed.
  • A traveller waits out Frost unless your stores can feed one more mouth, and their card tells you how much food that takes.
  • When Frost is two weeks away and food is short, Store food for Frost jumps ahead of beds and the roof.
  • Settlers carry stone to a kitchen or plan that's waiting for it, clear piles off a well, and carry the harvest off the garden.

Fortress building and planning6

  • Planning happens on the map. Plan shows what a building costs, you mark where it goes and press Build, and the materials are set aside when you mark it and handed back if you cancel.
  • Closing in a small room queues its roof, and inspecting a room shows how much of it is roofed and sheltered.
  • Floors can go under furniture, and a few buildings that showed the wrong picture look right.
  • Nothing can be built on top of the camp store. In testing, a wall around it starved a whole colony with full shelves.
  • Revealed minerals in Territory lists one row per seam, with how big it is, how far, which way and which level.
  • You can prepare a caravan, found a second colony, and send deliveries back and forth between your colonies.

Dungeons7

  • Auto-explore, click-to-walk and the stairs walk through shut dungeon doors and open them on the way. They used to say there was no path..knifee
  • Barrels in a new world's dungeons can be searched..knifee
  • Levers don't say "(searched)" when you hover over them any more..knifee
  • When you leave a floor, its spare keys are set down by the stairs, ready for when you come back..knifee
  • You can jam a pressure plate you've spotted. Stand next to it and press C, the controller's LT or click it, and it takes one turn..knifee
  • Wells are a rare find in a new world's dungeons now, one a floor at most. Bring water..knifee
  • When auto-explore runs out of floor, it tells you which locked door or lowered portcullis the rest is behind, and which way to go.

  • Repeated drops are counted, like "Vampiric Fang x2".danekjovaxutc5
  • Pets and hired soldiers get burned by fire, braziers and lava like you do. The Flame Salamander, Fire Phoenix and Ember Drake are fireproof, and their cards say so.thesquirrel_74982
  • F and Tab only aim at hostiles, so they stop telling you to shoot your escort.thesquirrel_74982
  • "Barely a bite" only shows up when the blow really did barely bite.danekjovaxutc5
  • The detailed log names the buff, like "You: Damage up" where it said "You: BUFF".nalathnidragon
  • Heals, cleanses and wards work on guards, townsfolk and farm animals.nalathnidragon
